# A household list you can talk to

- Public version: `v0.39.0`
- Face build: `0.171.0`
- Originally completed: `2026-10-18`
- Sanitized and published: `2026-10-18`

A walk-up tablet kiosk where anyone in the house adds, checks off or flags items by voice: fixed-rule parsing with quantities, merging and undo, urgent items that become Home tasks, and a Groceries row on the glasses.
